Privacy policy

Last updated 30 July 2026. Covers the soundcheck website and the iOS and Android apps.

The short version

  • We ask for an email address so you can have an account. Nothing else is required.
  • We do not sell your data, and there is no advertising network in the app.
  • Location and camera are optional, used only in the moment you tap the feature that needs them, and never in the background.
  • You can delete your account, and everything attached to it, at any time.

What we collect

  • Email address — to sign you in. We use a magic link, so there is no password for us to store or for anyone to steal.
  • Handle and display name — public, shown on your profile.
  • What you save — artists you mark as seen or want to see, nights you are interested in or going to, venues and people you follow.
  • Your gig diary — the nights you log: date, venue, line-up, your ratings and your notes.
  • Preferences — your city and your app settings.
  • Basic technical data — IP address and request time, held briefly for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location — only when you tap “Near me”, to sort cities and venues by distance. We use it in the moment and do not store your coordinates. Decline it and everything else still works; you pick your city manually.
  • Camera — only to scan another person’s profile QR code. The scan is decoded on your device and we open the profile it points to. No photo is taken, uploaded or kept.
  • Notifications — only if you opt in, to tell you when an artist you follow is announced.

soundcheck does not record audio, and does not read your photos, files or contacts.

Analytics

We use Umami to count page views. It sets no cookies, does no cross-site tracking, uses no advertising identifiers and builds no profile of you. We see which pages are popular, nothing more.

Music playback

Sets play through embedded YouTube and SoundCloud players. When a set plays, those services receive that request directly and their own privacy policies apply to it. We do not pass them your account details.

Listings

Line-ups, venues, dates, artist photos and biographies are compiled from publicly available sources. If you are an artist or a venue and want something corrected or removed, email us and we will fix it.

Who else handles your data

  • Neon — the database.
  • Render — runs the API.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub Pages — serve the site.
  • Resend — sends the sign-in emails.

These are infrastructure providers processing data on our instructions. We do not share your data for marketing, and we never sell it.

How long we keep it

Your account and diary stay until you delete them. Rate-limiting records are discarded within the hour. Cached listing data holds nothing about you.

Deleting your account

In the app: You → Settings → Delete account. It removes your profile, diary, follows, saved artists and sessions immediately and permanently. Full instructions, including how to request deletion without the app, are at soundcheck.club/delete-account.

Your rights

You can see everything we hold on your profile page, edit or delete any diary entry, and delete your account outright. If you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to a copy of your data and to object to processing — email us and we will action it.

Children

soundcheck lists club nights, which are age-restricted. It is not intended for anyone under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, for anything significant, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions, corrections or a deletion request: hello@soundcheck.club.
