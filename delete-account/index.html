Delete your account

Two ways to do it, and exactly what goes.

In the app

Open You, then Settings, then Delete account. You will be asked to confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email hello@soundcheck.club from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We action it within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account, permanently:

  • Your profile — email, handle, display name and avatar.
  • Your gig diary, including ratings and notes.
  • Artists you marked seen or wanted to see, and nights you saved or were going to.
  • Everyone and every venue you follow, and your followers.
  • Your reviews, playlists, preferences, notification tokens and sign-in sessions.

What stays

Event, artist and venue listings are not yours and are unaffected — they come from public sources. Nothing in them identifies you once your account is gone. Server logs holding an IP address are discarded on their own within the hour.

The full policy is at soundcheck.club/privacy.
